Quantum Composing

Help

How to play it

  1. Choose an interpretation, a basis, and an ensemble in the row under the title. They start as Copenhagen, computational basis, and By interpretation. Quantum Zeno shows watching, starting at 0.75. Decoherence shows γ, starting at 0.35. GRW collapse shows λ, starting at 0.3. Pilot-wave, and the random unitary basis, show seed, starting at 1905; the dice picks a new seed. ⇄ couplings turns the links between stations on or off. × closes that list. Three links start on: Spectra → harmony of Histories, Pair → melodies of Histories, and Orbits → drums of Histories. The other four start off. The italic line under that row is what the current interpretation does.
  2. I Spectra. Drag a phasor. Shared tones add as amplitudes, so the tone swells, thins, or beats. Use F, X, Z, T, or Tunnel to remix them. + spectrum adds one. Tap a harmonic of D along the bottom to measure it. Look collapses the cloud. Prepare again restores it. Let it ring plays it.
  3. II Pair. Tap inside an orbit to place or remove a note. Mirror, Reverse, Excite, Relax, and Precess change that orbit. Entangled starts on and ties each of those to the other particle; the row under the orbits sets the tie. Separable leaves the other particle alone. Orbit plays them. Bell pair prepares a pair and starts as correlated. Measure pair reads it. Teleport A → B sends A’s tune to B and clears A.
  4. III Histories. The blocks are sections. They start as α, β, γ, and ω. + section adds one. Each section has an operation on the Pair, one or more lengths in bars, and a ☠ slider: the chance the cat is found dead, in which case only the bass is left. + length adds a length. Harmony starts as a walk on the ratio lattice. Spread starts at 0.7. Tempo starts at 84. Live one history plays one measured history through those sections. forks is Sum or One. Sum plays every length of a section at once, louder where they agree, and the measured length cuts the rest with a tick. One plays only the measured length. measure tempo and measure loudness start off. half-life starts at ∞.
  5. IV Orbits. + ring adds a ring. Tap a step to cycle it: hit, superposed, GHZ cat, off. P is the chance of a superposed step. A ring can stay free or be tied to another ring, correlated or anti-correlated. Spin plays them. Open the box reads the state and says whether the cat is alive or dead.
  6. V Register. Pick a gate, then tap the grid. The gates are H, X, Y, Z, S, T, √X, R, CNOT, CZ, M, and erase. Time runs downward. Run plays the state. Tuning starts as Harmonic series. The instrument starts as glassbell. readout is the last hit of each pass; it starts as bowl. second pass is the instrument for the second time through; it starts as same instrument. + qubit and − qubit change the width. clear empties the grid.
  7. Orchestration, under the title. tune, inner, and bass set how loud each part sits. They start at 100, 58, and 72. tune double, inner double, and bass double start at Bare. They are Bare, Unison, or Octave: Unison and Octave add a second instrument. long notes starts at Restrike. It is Restrike or Held voice. Restrike repeats a note the instrument cannot hold. Held voice gives that line to an instrument that can. rescore starts at As scored. It is As scored, Assign by hand, or another ensemble. Assign by hand shows melody, counter-melody, counter-voice, chord, and bass voice. chairs starts at All at once. It is All at once or Admit one by one: bass and chord, then the inner parts, then the tune, then the drums.
  8. Physics, under Orchestration. forks starts at Sum. commutator starts at Off, which plays the notes as written. Excite then Mirror plays only the Pair notes that move when those two operations are swapped. H then Z does that for Register: Run plays the state that changes when H and Z are swapped, and it beats. Fifth, Third, and Quality play only the lattice chords that move under that step. A chord that does not move stays silent.
  9. Each station has its own volume slider. They start at 50. 50 is the opening loudness, and 100 is twice that loudness. 0 is silent. MIDI and WAV download that station. A WAV uses the slider as a fraction of the file’s peak: 50 is half of the loudest the file gets, and 100 is that full file, just under the peak. Starting play on one station stops the others. If the browser is holding the sound, tap the full-screen button that says Tap to enable sound.
