Help

How to play it

  1. Choose an interpretation, a basis, and an ensemble in the row under the title. ⇄ couplings turns the links between stations on or off. The italic line under that row is what the current interpretation does.
  2. I Spectra. Drag a phasor. Use F, X, Z, T, or Tunnel to remix them. + spectrum adds one. Tap a harmonic of D along the bottom to measure it. Look collapses the cloud. Prepare again restores it. Let it ring plays it.
  3. II Pair. Tap inside an orbit to place or remove a note. Entangled ties the two particles. Orbit plays them. Bell pair prepares a pair; Measure pair reads it. Teleport A → B sends A’s tune to B and clears A.
  4. III Histories. The blocks are sections. + section adds one. Set where the harmony comes from, the spread, and the tempo. Live one history plays it. measure tempo, measure loudness, and half-life change what gets measured along the way.
  5. IV Orbits. + ring adds a ring. Spin plays them. Open the box reads the state.
  6. V Register. Pick a gate, then tap the grid. Time runs downward. Run plays the state. The two menus choose the tuning and the instrument. + qubit and − qubit change the width. clear empties the grid.
  7. Each station has its own volume slider. They start at 80. MIDI and WAV download that station. Starting play on one station stops the others.
